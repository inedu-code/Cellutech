From Harmful Synthetics to Sustainable Bio-Composites

The Challenge: Product designers want to switch to sustainable materials but lack the data to know which bio-composites will perform comparably to synthetics — or where to source them at scale.

The CelluTech Solution: Our AI analyzes your product's entire lifecycle — from material properties to end-of-life disposal. It then matches you with the optimal bio-composite alternatives, sourced from agricultural waste fibers (hemp, flax, rice husk, coconut coir) and plant-based polymers (PLA, PHA, starch blends). You get performance data, sustainability metrics, and verified supplier connections — all in one platform.
